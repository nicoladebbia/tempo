Tempo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 25, 2026 | Last Updated: March 25, 2026

Tempo ("we", "us", "our") operates the Tempo: Life Operating System mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign in with Apple, we receive:

  • Apple User ID (unique identifier)
  • Email address (may be Apple's private relay address)
  • Display name (if you choose to share it)

1.2 Health & Fitness Data

With your explicit consent, we access data from:

  • Apple HealthKit: Steps, active energy, heart rate, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep analysis, workouts
  • Whoop: Recovery score, sleep performance, strain, HRV, resting heart rate, body measurements (via Whoop API with your OAuth authorization)

Important: Raw HealthKit data never leaves your device. Only aggregate scores are stored in our database.

1.3 Nutrition Data

Via NutriTrack integration: meals logged, calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat totals.

1.4 Academic & Productivity Data

Study session durations, focus timer usage, non-negotiable completion tracking.

1.5 Calendar Data

Read-only access to your calendar for schedule-aware training recommendations. Calendar data is processed locally and never transmitted to our servers.

1.6 Social & Gamification Data

Username, display name, XP totals, level, achievements, friend connections, challenge participation.

1.7 Device & Usage Data

Device token (for push notifications), app version, crash reports, anonymized behavioral analytics (screens viewed, features used), locale, and timezone.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Core app functionality: Generating training plans, recovery prescriptions, accountability tracking, and daily scores
  • AI-powered insights: With your separate, explicit consent, anonymized aggregate scores are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate personalized recommendations. No personally identifiable information is included.
  • Analytics: Understanding feature usage to improve the app (via PostHog, with your consent)
  • Push notifications: Sending accountability reminders and updates you've opted into

4. AI Data Processing

Tempo uses Anthropic's Claude AI to generate personalized insights. This requires separate, explicit consent during onboarding (not bundled with other permissions).

  • Only anonymized aggregate scores are sent (recovery percentages, sleep hours, calorie totals)
  • No names, email addresses, Apple IDs, or device identifiers are sent
  • Per Anthropic's API Terms, your data is NOT used to train AI models
  • Data retained by Anthropic for up to 30 days
  • You can disable AI features at any time: Settings > Privacy > AI Features
  • The app works fully without AI consent using template-based alternatives

5. HealthKit Data

We comply with Apple's HealthKit guidelines:

  • HealthKit data is never stored in iCloud
  • HealthKit data is never sold or shared for advertising
  • HealthKit data is used solely for app functionality
  • Raw HealthKit samples never leave your device
  • You can revoke HealthKit access at any time via iOS Settings > Privacy > Health

7. Your Rights

For All Users

  • Access: Export all your data in JSON format (Settings > Privacy > Export My Data)
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data (Settings > Account > Delete Account). 7-day cooling-off period, 30-day processing.
  • Correction: Update your username, display name, and profile
  • Portability: JSON export in standard format
  • Restrict: Disconnect individual integrations without deleting your account
  • Withdraw consent: Revoke HealthKit, Whoop, NutriTrack, AI, or analytics consent at any time

GDPR Rights (EU Users)

Lawful basis: Contract for core service; Explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)) for health data processing. Lead supervisory authority: Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali (Italian DPA). Breach notification within 72 hours per GDPR Art. 33.

CCPA/CPRA Rights (California Users)

Right to Know, Right to Delete, Right to Opt-Out of Sale (not applicable -- we do not sell data), Right to Non-Discrimination.

8. Data Security

  • All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Authentication via Sign in with Apple with JWT (ES256)
  • Keychain storage for sensitive credentials
  • No passwords stored -- Apple handles authentication

9. Children's Privacy

Tempo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. Medical Disclaimer

Tempo is NOT a medical device and does NOT provide medical advice. Training recommendations and recovery prescriptions are informational only. Always consult a healthcare professional before making health decisions.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification and update the "Last Updated" date.
