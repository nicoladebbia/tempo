## Description

Your body. Your mind. Your schedule. One system.

Tempo is the life operating system that unifies fitness, recovery, nutrition, study, and accountability into a single command center. No more switching between 5 apps. No more guessing if you should train hard or rest. No more wasted evenings wondering where the day went.

Tempo tells you exactly what to do -- and holds you accountable until you do it.

HOW IT WORKS

Tempo reads your biometric data from Whoop and Apple Health, your nutrition from NutriTrack, and your calendar. Then it builds your day:

REPFORGE -- INTELLIGENT TRAINING
Your workouts adapt to your body. Tempo adjusts every session based on your Whoop recovery score, sleep quality, and upcoming schedule. Green recovery? Push for PRs. Red recovery? Mobility and light work. Football tomorrow? No heavy legs today. Progressive overload tracked automatically across weeks.

RECOVERIQ -- DAILY PRESCRIPTIONS
More than metrics -- actionable prescriptions. Wake up to your recovery score plus a clear plan: training intensity, meal timing, target bedtime, hydration goal, and caffeine cutoff. Spot trends before they become problems with 7/30/90-day analysis.

LOCKDOWN -- DRILL SERGEANT ACCOUNTABILITY
Set your daily non-negotiables: study hours, training, meals. Tempo tracks completion automatically through integrations -- no self-reporting honor system. Miss your targets? Notifications escalate from gentle reminders to drill-sergeant mode as the evening approaches. Earn your leisure time.

CLUTCHTIME -- COMPETITIVE ARENA
XP for every workout completed, every meal logged, every study target hit. Weekly leaderboards with friends. Custom challenges. Achievements and streaks. Turn discipline into a game -- with real performance data, not check boxes.

LIFEOS -- UNIFIED DASHBOARD
See your entire day in one glance. Four quadrants -- Body, Fuel, Mind, Move -- each pulling live data from your integrations. AI-powered weekly reports reveal patterns you would never spot on your own: how your sleep affects your training, how your nutrition impacts your recovery.

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE

- Whoop Integration: Recovery, sleep, strain, HRV -- all flowing into your daily plan
- Apple Health: Steps, heart rate, workouts synced automatically
- Focus Timer: Built-in Pomodoro timer for deep study sessions
- Smart Notifications: Escalating accountability that adapts to your schedule
- Offline First: Works without internet. Syncs when connected.
- Privacy First: Your data stays yours. Sign in with Apple. No ads. No data selling.

WHO IS TEMPO FOR?

Tempo is for anyone who refuses to be average. University students balancing academics and athletics. Gym-goers who want their training to match their recovery. Anyone who has tried 5 different apps and wished they talked to each other. If you want one system that tracks everything and tolerates zero excuses -- Tempo is it.

Stop managing your life across a dozen apps. Start operating it from one.

Download Tempo. Build the system. Trust the process.
